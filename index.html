How to Calculate CGPA in Bangladesh Universities

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is how most universities in Bangladesh measure overall academic performance. Each course contributes in proportion to its credit hours: a 3-credit course counts three times as much as a 1-credit course. To find your CGPA, multiply the grade point of each course by its credit hours, add all of those values together, and divide by your total credit hours.

This calculator does that arithmetic for you, semester by semester, on the standard 4.00 grading system used by most universities in Bangladesh — or on the NSU, BRAC, UIU or AIUB scale if you select one.

Supported University Grading Systems

This CGPA calculator supports both the commonly used Bangladesh Standard Scale and selected university-specific grading systems. If your university follows a different grading structure, select your university before calculating.

  • Standard Scale — most universities in Bangladesh
  • North South University (NSU) — no A+, top grade A (4.00) from 93 marks
  • BRAC University — A+ and A both carry 4.00, with a 13-step scale down to D−
  • American International University–Bangladesh (AIUB) — 9-step scale, no A− or B−
  • United International University (UIU) — A+ from 90 marks, D at 1.75

If your university is not listed, use the Standard Scale unless your institution officially follows a different grading policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is a measure of your overall academic performance across multiple semesters. It is the credit-weighted average of all the grade points you have earned.

2. How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA = total grade points earned ÷ total credit hours. Each course’s grade point is multiplied by its credit hours first, so higher-credit courses affect the result more.

3. Is this CGPA calculator accurate?

Yes, for the scale you select. It follows the standard 4.00 grading system commonly used in Bangladesh universities, and the NSU, BRAC, UIU and AIUB scales as commonly published. The result is only as accurate as the grades and credits you enter — double-check them against your transcript.

4. Can I calculate CGPA for multiple semesters?

Yes. Add a semester card for each semester and enter its courses. The calculator shows each semester GPA separately and your cumulative CGPA in the bottom bar.

5. What grading scale does this calculator use?

By default, the Bangladesh Standard Scale (A+ = 4.00, A = 3.75, A− = 3.50, B+ = 3.25, B = 3.00, and so on). You can switch to NSU, BRAC, UIU or AIUB from the grading-system card.

6. Can I calculate semester GPA separately?

Yes. The calculator automatically shows the GPA for each semester in that semester’s header, alongside your overall cumulative CGPA.

7. Does this calculator support retake or improvement courses?

No. Retake and improvement calculations are not included. Results are based on the standard course grades and credit hours you enter.

8. What is a good CGPA in Bangladesh?

A CGPA above 3.50 is generally considered very good in most Bangladeshi universities, while a CGPA of 3.75 or above is considered excellent.

9. How much CGPA is required for scholarships?

Scholarship requirements vary by university and program. Many scholarships ask for a CGPA between 3.50 and 4.00 — check the specific scholarship’s official requirements.

10. Can I use this calculator on mobile?

Yes. The calculator is fully responsive and works on mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices. Your entries are saved in your browser, so a refresh keeps them.

11. Does this calculator support NSU CGPA calculation?

Yes. Select NSU before entering your courses and the calculator applies the NSU grading scale, where the top grade is A (4.00) from 93 marks and there is no A+.

12. How do I calculate CGPA for BRAC University?

Select BRAC University from the university selector before entering course grades and credits. The calculator automatically applies BRAC’s grading scale.

13. Can I calculate CGPA for AIUB?

Yes. Select AIUB and calculate semester GPA and cumulative CGPA using its grading structure — A+ (4.00) from 90 marks, with no A− or B− grade.

14. Can UIU students use this CGPA calculator?

Yes. Select United International University (UIU) to calculate GPA and CGPA using UIU’s grading scale, where A+ starts at 90 marks.
